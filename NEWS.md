## 6. VOYAGER 1 — one light-day from Earth, a first
- NASA's Voyager 1 reached one light-day from Earth on Wed 18 Nov 2026 at 5:16 a.m.
  ET — about 16 billion miles (26 billion km). First human-made object to reach
  that distance; its radio signals now take a full 24 hours to reach Earth.
